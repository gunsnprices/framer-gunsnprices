# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First version.

- CMS: syncs a managed collection of calibers (293) or sample guns and ammo (2,841 products), with a choice of
  types, number of items and fields. Optional "Price page" link field, off by default.
- "Sync" in the CMS repeats the saved settings without opening the panel.
- Asks before removing items or fields from a collection.
- Canvas: adds the cost per round of an ammo box as a text layer, in four formats.
- Data: GunsNPrices catalog snapshot of 2026-10-12, CC BY 4.0. No network requests.
